❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does the review process take?
A: Initial screening takes 5-7 days. If selected for research, the full process takes 2-3 months.

Q: What if my idea is rejected?
A: We provide feedback on why your idea wasn't selected and encourage reapplication with improvements.

Q: Can I submit multiple ideas?
A: Yes! You can submit as many ideas as you'd like. Each will be evaluated independently.

Q: What happens when I'm approved for funding?
A: We present your opportunity to our investor network and facilitate introductions.
